FAQ

1. Whats the point?

Personally, whenever I want to create and upload content I'd struggle transferring over 25 gb of videos to my deskstop for editing.

Issues would arise and every website I'd go to would require me to pay for anything over 25 gb, the free ones would take ages to upload.

This is why the basic feature includes a limit of 25 gb, because I just could not find a way to transfer 25 gb+ without using CMD (Command line Interface), and I believe that you should be able to solve your problem without wasting time on learning how to transfer through other technical means.

2. Privacy Concerns

Your data is safe, your passwords are never saved so if you do forget them you'll have to reset it as we do not store passwords, your content is also safe and is hashed inside our database so no one can see what that image, video, text is unless it's you on your board.

Now obviously if you do make your board public anyone can see when they go to your link, although every board starts off as private, so you do have that option later on to show off your content.

3. Why Choose Us?

You can upload almost anything, not just files. Most websites just have you limited on files to transfer, which is cool but we have expanded on that idea, and now you are able to upload Links, Videos, Images, and Text (More to be added soon). Not only that you have a sense of personalization to YOUR board.
